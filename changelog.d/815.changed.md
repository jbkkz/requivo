- Discovery reaches documents in fewer rounds (#815). A turn may now carry up to nine questions, up from six, and the engine prompt and the Claude Code plugin reasoning guide ask every question above the information-value bar in the same turn, reading the cap from the contract instead of restating "3 to 6", so no high-value question is held back for a later round and none is padded in to reach a count. They name filler and say not to ask it: a question no plausible answer to which would move the estimate, the architecture or what gets delivered (a generic success-metric question, a nice-to-have wish list) becomes an `inferred` default the client can veto. After an answer settles the part of a high-impact topic that changes the solution, the engine lowers what is left to an assumption instead of asking again.
- Readiness means "nothing left that would change the solution", and a session can no longer stop "Not ready" with nothing to ask (#815). A high-impact topic the client delegated ("you decide", evidence opening "Client delegated") no longer blocks, whatever confidence or completeness the turn gave it, and neither does one holding a default that no question of the turn still asks about: it is an assumption offered for veto. A blank topic that still blocks and that the turn left unasked gets the probe from the schema as its question, on every surface that lists questions (`status --json`, the terminal, the CLI loop, the Web). Compatibility: compatible - `status --json` keeps its shape; `readiness` moves toward ready only, `questions` may now hold a probe the turn did not write, and a Requivo older than this one refuses a model carrying seven to nine questions.
- The interactive checkpoint (`requivo run`, `requivo discover` at a terminal, and the `/requivo:run` skill) now asks every question a turn returned, one at a time, before folding the answers into one turn, instead of the first four (#815): a question held back cost another paid turn and another round trip. A blocker missing from an older session, a topic added to the schema since, gets its probe asked too.
