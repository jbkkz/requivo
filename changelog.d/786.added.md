- The software schema gains `existing_solutions` in its *Why?* pillar (#786), required, default impact high: what is already in place or paid for, the off-the-shelf options considered and why they were rejected, and the budget envelope (which leaves the `constraints` probe). Build vs buy can delete the project, so the engine asks it in the first turn instead of waiting for the client to volunteer it; the decision brief can contest building at all, and the estimate goes stale when the answer changes. The two browsable examples (and the `requivo demo` payload) carry it as not yet asked, so the leave-approval brief now reads as a draft.
- Compatibility: compatible - a software session written before this change still loads, verifies and reports status; `existing_solutions` reads at its baseline impact, so it is listed among the blocking slots (and a session that was ready reports not ready) until the next turn fills it. One observable moved: a software proposal 3.5.0 accepted without the new slots is refused by `model apply`/`model diff` as `missing_required_slot`, exit 0 → 1.
