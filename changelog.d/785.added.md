- `requivo status --recap` says where a session stands on return (#785): what is decided (the confirmed topics with their values, and the recorded decisions), what is open (the questions, the defaults assumed on the client's behalf, the decisions proposed for them to own, the bets still to test with their test plans, and whatever still blocks readiness, so a turn that proposed no question is never reported as having nothing open), what changed since the oldest current document was last generated, named with its revision (moved topics and moved decisions, challenges, exclusions or thresholds), which documents are stale and on which topics, and the one next step. It is computed offline from the model, its revision history and the recorded stale flags, never from a provider, in the model's own words rather than slot ids, percentages or confidence labels. `--recap --json` gives the same recap plus `next`; a bare `model.json` is refused, having no history. `requivo run <slug>` prints a short form before resuming, leaving out the verdict and the open items its checkpoint prints next.
- `requivo status` now lists each generated document as up to date or *needs updating, not current*, next to its name, so a stale document can no longer be read as current from the status view (#785).
- Compatibility: compatible - a new flag and a new `--json` form; `status`'s text gains a `DOCUMENTS` section and `run <slug>` prints the recap first.
