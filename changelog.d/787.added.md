- The perimeter router's verdict is remembered (#787). `session.json` gains `perimeter_fit` (`fits` or `no_fit`) and `perimeter_fit_reason`, written when `discover` routes a new session and `null` otherwise. A `no_fit` session still continues under `software`, but now says so where it matters: one `Perimeter` line in `requivo status` (and `perimeter_fit` / `perimeter_fit_reason` in `status --json` and `session show --json`), a warning on stderr before every generator verb runs — a warning, never a refusal — and a note under the title of every document saved for that session.
- Compatibility: compatible - two optional fields, `null` for every existing session; an older Requivo carries them through a re-save as unknown keys.
