- The estimate is now a number a client can budget against (#821): its total adds the delivery work no story is — setup and hosting, design, QA and acceptance testing, project management, rollout, migration and training — each with its basis, beside a day range per story with the basis of that figure. It says what one day covers, which team is assumed and what is left out; which path it prices — the one the analysis recommends, buying or configuring included, the stories being that path's work — and the alternative's own rough figure and any recurring licence; what it costs at a day rate, stated or assumed, and what a one-off budget the model states buys of it, computed by Requivo rather than by the model; and the questions whose answers would move the total, each with the days it adds or removes. Unresolved topics no question prices are still listed. The keyless `/requivo:estimate` writes the same document.
- The user stories and the PRD carry what a developer builds from (#821): each story may hold developer notes (the data it touches, the integration contract it relies on and its failure handling, a constraint specific to it), and a build gets as many stories as it needs, sliced by behaviour, platform work being the estimate's; the PRD gains a data model, a proposed technical approach for the hardest problems (the mechanism, how a failure is detected and recovered, how it is verified) and non-functional requirements, writes each integration as a contract and its edge cases with their failure modes, and says when the analysis leans towards buying, its requirements then doubling as the evaluation checklist. When it does, the acceptance criteria verify the tool as configured rather than a build's internals. The keyless `/requivo:stories`, `/requivo:prd` and `/requivo:criteria` follow. The four prompts changed, so their golden baselines are re-captured separately.
- Compatibility: compatible - every new field is optional and stays out of the `--json` payloads while empty, so a document written by an older Requivo loads and dumps as before; the estimate's Markdown replaces its "Drives" column and "Notes" list with a "Basis" column.
