- The estimate is now a number a client can budget against (#821): its total adds the delivery work no story is — setup and hosting, design, QA and acceptance testing, project management, rollout, migration and training — each with its basis, beside a day range per story with the basis of that figure. It says what one day covers, which team is assumed and what is left out; which path it prices and what would replace it (the build path when the analysis leans towards buying); what it costs at a day rate, stated or assumed, and what a one-off budget the model states buys of it, computed by Requivo rather than by the model; and the questions whose answers would move the total, each with the days it adds or removes. Unresolved topics no question prices are still listed. The keyless `/requivo:estimate` writes the same document.
- The user stories and the PRD carry what a developer builds from (#821): each story may hold developer notes (the data it touches, the integration contract it relies on and its failure handling, a constraint specific to it), and a build gets as many stories as it needs, sliced by behaviour, platform work being the estimate's; the PRD gains a data model and non-functional requirements, writes each integration as a contract and its edge cases with their failure modes, and says when the analysis leans towards buying, its requirements then doubling as the evaluation checklist. The keyless `/requivo:stories` and `/requivo:prd` follow. The three prompts changed, so their golden baselines are re-captured separately.
- Compatibility: compatible - every new field is optional and stays out of the `--json` payloads while empty, so a document written by an older Requivo loads and dumps as before; the estimate's Markdown replaces its "Drives" column and "Notes" list with a "Basis" column.
