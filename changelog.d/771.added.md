- `requivo status` shows the central idea (#771): per-pillar progress (each pillar's mean completeness) on every checkpoint, and each question with its information value, `uncertainty × impact` computed by the core from the slot it targets. The core now ranks the questions by that value (a tie keeps the order the turn proposed), and every surface reads that one order: the terminal, `status --json`, Requivo Web, and the interactive loop, whose checkpoint therefore asks the questions most worth asking. `status --json` gains `pillars` and an `information_value` on each question. The framework text (`requivo schema --framework`) now says how the order is computed and what happens to the questions a checkpoint does not ask: they are not queued, and the next turn derives its own.
- Compatibility: compatible - additive `--json` fields; the order of `status --json`'s `questions` moved from the turn's own to the ranked one, named in `docs/compatibility.md`.
