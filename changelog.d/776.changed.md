- Discovery no longer spends a question on what a product context card already states about the requester — their own time, launch horizon or how they will judge the result (#776). The engine fills the slot from the card, cites it as `Context card:`, and asks for a number the card leaves out only when the request's scope turns on it. On ten solo-builder product ideas with a builder card, two runs per side, the launch horizon was re-asked in 0 runs instead of 1–3, and the budget it freed went to whether users would switch from what they use today (7–8 runs instead of 3–4).
