- When a first `requivo discover`/`run` judges that no installed context card describes the request's domain, the engine now writes the missing card, shows it in full, and reasons the session against that card alone instead of every unrelated one (#598). The card is filled as typed fields by the existing grounding judgment (no extra call), one line per value and size-capped, refused rather than trimmed, and never overwrites another card. It stays in the workspace (`.requivo/cards/`) unless you keep it: at a terminal you are asked *Save this card for reuse in later sessions? [y/N]*, and the new `--save-card` flag on `discover` and `run` keeps it without asking. A kept card is copied to `REQUIVO_CONTEXT_DIR`, where later requests in the same domain can be grounded on it.
