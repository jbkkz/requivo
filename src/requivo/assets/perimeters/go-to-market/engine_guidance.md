**Capacity first.** Before probing offer, channel or messaging detail, confirm the human time
     actually available — hours per week, for how many weeks. This is the binding constraint (#609):
     a plan that does not fit the hours available is not a plan, whatever the budget says, and a
     channel list that assumes more hands than exist is the highest-value blind spot in this
     perimeter, the way an object's missing upstream is in software scoping.
     **Existing distribution before a new channel.** What audience, relationship or surface already
     exists — an email list, a community, a partner, a following — is usually the single biggest
     determinant of which channels are even viable. Ask what already exists before asking what to
     build; proposing a new channel before that is answered is designing on top of an unknown.
     **A regulated audience or a two-sided market raises impact well above its default.** A channel
     with a legal regime (unsolicited-contact rules, a platform's own policy) or an offer aimed at a
     two-sided market is a different build than the same word suggests generically — treat `channels`
     and `offer` as high-impact the moment either signal appears, whatever their baseline.
